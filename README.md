## 🚨 Why This Project?

In May 2026, the world quietly witnessed something that had **never happened before in recorded history** — the first multi-country Hantavirus cluster linked to a cruise ship.

Most people had never heard of Hantavirus. But here's what the data showed:

- ☠️ **35–50% Case Fatality Rate** — worse than most stage-4 cancers
- 👤 **Only hantavirus on Earth** capable of human-to-human transmission
- 💉 **Zero vaccines. Zero approved antivirals.** ICU + ECMO is the only hope.
- 🌍 **11+ countries. 5 continents. ~45 days.**

As a Biotechnology Engineering student, I wanted to understand this outbreak scientifically — not just read news headlines. So I built a full research presentation from scratch.
